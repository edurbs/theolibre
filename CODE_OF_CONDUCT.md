# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The TheoLibre project is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who participates in the project.

Participation in TheoLibre is open to people with different backgrounds, technical experience, cultures, languages, and viewpoints.

We expect all contributors and community members to treat one another with respect and professionalism.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

* Being respectful and considerate of others.
* Assuming good intentions while remaining open to correction.
* Giving and receiving constructive feedback.
* Discussing technical disagreements objectively.
* Respecting different levels of technical experience.
* Keeping discussions focused on the project.
* Accepting decisions made by project maintainers after reasonable discussion.
* Helping newcomers understand the project.
* Respecting the licenses and intellectual-property rights of others.
* Reporting problems through the appropriate project channels.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
* Discriminatory or hateful comments.
* Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
* Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual attention.
* Publishing someone's private or personal information without permission.
* Impersonating project maintainers or other contributors.
* Deliberately submitting malicious code or content.
* Deliberately introducing vulnerabilities or backdoors.
* Repeatedly disrupting project discussions or development.
* Deliberately violating the project's license or contribution policies.
* Attempting to use the project or its community to facilitate unauthorized commercial exploitation.
* Any other conduct that would reasonably make the project community unsafe or hostile.

## Technical Disagreements

Technical disagreement is expected in an open-source-style development community.

Disagreement about:

* architecture;
* programming languages;
* frameworks;
* user-interface design;
* implementation details;
* project priorities; or
* feature requests

is not, by itself, a violation of this Code of Conduct.

Participants should focus criticism on ideas, implementations, and technical decisions rather than on individuals.

Maintainers may close discussions that have become repetitive, hostile, or unproductive.

## Project Scope and Licensing

TheoLibre is source-available software distributed under the **PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0**.

Community members are expected to respect the project's licensing restrictions.

In particular, contributors must not intentionally use contributions, forks, or project infrastructure to facilitate unauthorized commercial use of TheoLibre.

For licensing questions, consult the [`LICENSE.md`](LICENSE.md) file and [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the circumstances, maintainers may:

1. Ask a participant to modify or stop inappropriate behavior.
2. Remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other content.
3. Close discussions or disable participation in a particular thread.
4. Temporarily restrict participation in the project.
5. Permanently ban a participant from project spaces.
6. Take other reasonable action necessary to protect the project and its community.

The response will depend on the severity and circumstances of the violation.

Maintainers may take action even when conduct occurs outside a project discussion if it has a direct and significant impact on the safety or functioning of the TheoLibre community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers.

For sensitive matters, use a private communication channel rather than creating a public GitHub issue.

When reporting an incident, provide:

* A description of what happened.
* The relevant date and time.
* Links or other evidence when available.
* The GitHub username or identity of the people involved.
* Any other information that may help the maintainers understand the situation.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## False or Malicious Reports

Good-faith reports are welcome, even when an investigation does not ultimately find a violation.

Knowingly submitting false accusations, fabricating evidence, or abusing the reporting process to harass another participant is itself unacceptable behavior.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers should:

* Apply this Code of Conduct consistently.
* Avoid unnecessary favoritism.
* Give participants an opportunity to explain relevant circumstances when appropriate.
* Protect the privacy of people involved in sensitive reports.
* Take reasonable steps to prevent retaliation against people who make good-faith reports.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by established open-source community standards, including the Contributor Covenant and other community governance practices.

It is intentionally adapted for the TheoLibre project and is not intended to replace the project's license or other legal policies.

## Changes to This Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct may be updated as the TheoLibre community evolves.

Significant changes should be communicated through the project's normal communication channels.

## Contact

For questions or private reports concerning this Code of Conduct, contact the TheoLibre project maintainers through the contact information provided in the repository.

Thank you for helping keep TheoLibre a respectful, constructive, and collaborative project.
